A LITTLE PLACE FOR YOU — DREAMY BLUE EDITION

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the little-website folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html with Live Server.

MUSIC
The five songs are included in assets/audio and arranged in the playlist order.
Click a track button to play it. You can also use the audio player's controls.
The next track starts when the current song ends. Keep the assets folder alongside index.html.

PERSONALIZE
- Edit the name in the heading in index.html.
- Edit the note text in the letter section in index.html.
- Replace gallery placeholders with your own photos if you want.

The website keeps its original sections, dreamy blue styling, stickers, little messages, question, surprise, and letter.
